# P2-07 · Bid Risk and Qualifications

**Builds on:** P2-03 (obligations, scope matrix), P2-06 (clarifications, qualification proposals), P2-04/P2-05 (cost and labour for impact). **Needs:** the company scope-gap checklist and risk-appetite guidance (business track).

Begin by replying with a plan that maps each item under **Done when** to the work that satisfies it. Wait for approval before changing files.

## Read first

- `docs/plan/project-context.md`: *proposal*, *evidence record*, guardrail 3.
- Requirements: §6.13 (FR-RSK-01 to 06), §5 (G3), §3 (RACI: assumptions, risk allowances).
- `docs/plan/BUILD_LOG.md`.

## Goal

Give the bid team a complete, evidence-backed view of scope gaps, design-responsibility exposure and execution risk. Each risk has a proposed treatment and cost impact. The assumptions, exclusions and qualifications list ties every entry back to its source.

## Scope

FR-RSK-01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 06.

## Build

1. **Scope-gap checklist** (FR-RSK-01): a configurable checklist per system covering:
   - pumps, tanks, breeching inlets, hydrants, hose reels;
   - hydraulic calculations, shop drawings, testing and commissioning;
   - authority inspections and FSC support;
   - builder's works, power supply interfaces.

   Pre-fill statuses from the scope matrix and takeoff. Each item must end as included, excluded, by others or clarified.
2. **Design responsibility** (FR-RSK-02): detect design-and-build, shop-drawing, hydraulic-calculation and QP-engagement obligations from spec and contract text, with citations. Present them as risks with a proposed treatment.
3. **Execution risks** (FR-RSK-03): flag basement levels, installation heights above a threshold, occupied or live building notes, night-work and shutdown clauses, high-rise logistics, congested ceilings and work at height. Evidence comes from drawings (levels, sections), spec and bid parameters.
4. **Impact** (FR-RSK-04): a cost and programme impact range or allowance per risk, computed with the P2-04/P2-05 engines where possible. The estimator accepts or adjusts each one, with a reason.
5. **Qualifications list** (FR-RSK-05): proposed assumptions, exclusions, qualifications and deviations drawn from:
   - risks, scope-matrix rows, unresolved clarifications (P2-06);
   - measurement conventions (P1-09).

   Each entry links to its source item and is editable, with history.
6. **Risk register** (FR-RSK-06): owner, treatment (price, qualify, clarify, accept), status, and links to evidence and qualification entries.
7. **G3 readiness:** a check that every checklist item is resolved and every risk has a treatment. It is shown to the Commercial Director before approval.

## Done when

- The checklist for a synthetic bid pre-fills from the scope matrix, and the G3 readiness check fails until every item is resolved. Tagged FR-RSK-01.
- A fixture spec with design-and-build and hydraulic-calculation clauses yields cited design-responsibility risks. Tagged FR-RSK-02.
- Seeded execution conditions (basement, high ceilings, night work) are flagged with evidence. Tagged FR-RSK-03.
- Impact values compute from the engines, and estimator adjustments record reasons. Tagged FR-RSK-04.
- Every qualification entry links to a source item. Tagged FR-RSK-05.
- The register tracks owner, treatment and status, with audit history. Tagged FR-RSK-06.
- A build log entry is appended.
