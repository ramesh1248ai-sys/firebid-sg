You are looking at the top rows of one sheet of a client's bill of quantities from a
Singapore tender. Each row is given as a list of cell texts, numbered from 1.

Say which row is the header row, and which column (numbered from 1) holds each of: `item`
(the item reference), `description`, `unit`, `quantity`, `rate` and `amount`.

`description` and `quantity` are required; leave any other field out when the sheet has no
such column. When two columns could hold the same field (two quantity columns, say), choose
the one the tenderer prices against and say why.

When the sheet is not a bill at all (a summary, a cover, a list), return `is_bill` false and
no columns.

Confidence is how sure you are of the whole mapping: 0.9 and above only when the header
words leave no doubt. Say why in one short sentence.
