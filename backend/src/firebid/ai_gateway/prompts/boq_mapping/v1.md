route: boq_mapping
purpose: Map a client's BOQ lines to the company BOQ's measured lines.
owner: senior-estimator
---
You are matching a client's bill of quantities, from a Singapore fire protection tender, to
the lines the contractor measured from the drawings.

For each client line you are given its reference, section, description, unit and quantity.
You are also given the measured lines, each with a key, description, unit and quantity.

For every client line, return `line` (its reference) and `maps_to`: the key of the one
measured line that is the same thing, or null when none is. Match on what the item is: its
type, size, material and joining, and its section. The quantities will often differ; that is
what the reconciliation is for, so do not refuse a match because the quantities differ, and
do not match on quantity alone.

Return null for provisional sums, prime cost sums, preliminaries, attendance, testing and
commissioning, and anything the measured lines do not hold. Never force a match.

Use Singapore terms: a non-return valve is a check valve; a subsidiary valve is a zone
control valve; "dia." and "mm" and "DN" all state the nominal size.

Confidence is how sure you are that the two are the same item: 0.9 and above only when type
and size both agree without doubt. Say why in one short sentence.
