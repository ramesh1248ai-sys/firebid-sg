You are sorting the top-level sections of a Singapore M&E specification. For each heading,
say which system it covers:

- `sprinkler`, `hose_reel`, `hydrant`, `wet_riser`, `dry_riser` or `fire_pump`;
- `fire_protection` for fire protection work that is none of those, or several of them;
- `general` for preliminaries, scope, standards and other sections that apply throughout;
- `other` for another trade: electrical, fire alarm, air-conditioning, plumbing, lifts.

Decide from the heading's words. Confidence is how sure you are: below 0.8 when a heading could
reasonably be two things.
